Beans & Legumes

Heirloom Bean and Sweet Corn Succotash

Shell beans and late-summer sweet corn simmer together in a buttery, thyme-scented broth. Succotash is the oldest farmhouse supper in North America, and it costs almost nothing when the garden is producing.

Total 1 hr 5 min Main Dish

Beans & Legumes

Sour Dill Wax Beans (Sauer Dill Bohnen)

Yellow wax beans blanched, then tossed in a warm sweet-sour dill sauce thickened with a roux. A classic Bavarian side for roast pork, sausages and new potatoes.

Total 35 min Side Dish

Cabbage & Brassicas

Charred Cabbage Wedges with Anchovy Butter

Pointed cabbage wedges seared hard in a cast-iron pan until the leaves blacken and sweeten, then finished with anchovy butter and lemon. A winter vegetable with real drama.

Total 35 min Side Dish

Eggplant 2 recipes

Eggplant

Hunan-Style Smashed Eggplant

Whole eggplants grilled until the skin blisters and the interior collapses, then opened up and dressed with garlic, chile, black vinegar and sesame. The easiest way to cook eggplant and the most flavourful.

Total 40 min Side Dish

Eggplant

Sicilian Eggplant Caponata

Fried eggplant folded into a sweet-and-sour agrodolce of celery, onion, olives and capers. Caponata is a pantry dish that tastes best after a night in the refrigerator.

Total 1 hr Side Dish

Okra & Southern 1 recipe

Okra & Southern

Blistered Okra with Garlic and Smoked Paprika

Whole okra pods left intact, blistered in a hot pan or on the grill with garlic, cumin and smoked paprika. High heat and dry pods are all it takes to banish the slime.

Total 20 min Side Dish

Peppers 1 recipe

Peppers

Quinoa and Herb Stuffed Sweet Peppers

Halved sweet heirloom peppers filled with herbed quinoa, tomato and feta, then baked until the edges caramelise. Vegetarian, freezer-friendly, and easy to scale for a crowd.

Total 1 hr 5 min Main Dish

Preserves & Condiments

Ground Cherry and Vanilla Jam

Husk cherries have a floral, pineapple-and-vanilla flavour that makes an extraordinary jam. A small-batch preserve that sets naturally from the fruit's own pectin.

Total 55 min Preserve

Preserves & Condiments

Wild Huckleberry Jam

Wild huckleberries or blueberries cooked down with lemon and a touch of cinnamon into a dark, intensely flavoured jam. A small batch that rewards careful skimming and patient simmering.

Total 55 min Preserve

Preserves & Condiments

Charred Corn and Sweet Pepper Relish

Blistered corn kernels, sweet peppers and onion in a bright cider vinaigrette. Spoon it over grilled meat, fold it into tacos, or serve it with chips as a smoky alternative to tomato salsa.

Total 23 min Condiment

Root Vegetables 1 recipe

Root Vegetables

Roasted Beets with Mint Yogurt

Whole beets roasted in a foil parcel until they collapse, then sliced over cool mint-flecked yogurt with pistachios and orange. Sweet, earthy and completely unlike boiled beets.

Total 1 hr 15 min Side Dish

Soups & Stews 2 recipes

Soups & Stews

Garden Sweet Corn Chowder with Chives

Sweet corn, potato and onion are simmered in their own starch and blended just enough to turn creamy. No cream needed, and the flavour of heirloom corn comes through unmasked.

Total 55 min Main Dish

Soups & Stews

Ajoblanco (White Gazpacho with Almonds and Grapes)

Bread, almonds, garlic and sherry vinegar blended into a chilled white soup and finished with sweet grapes. Ajoblanco predates tomato gazpacho and takes fifteen minutes to make.

Total 2 hr 15 min Soup

Squash 2 recipes

Squash

White Pumpkin Pie with Brown Butter

White heirloom pumpkins roasted until caramelised, pureed, and baked into a custard with brown butter and warm spice. Deeper and less watery than anything from a can.

Total 5 hr Dessert

Squash

Zucchini and Feta Fritters

Grated zucchini squeezed dry, bound with egg, flour, dill and crumbled feta, then pan-fried into lacy-edged cakes. The most useful recipe we know for a glut of summer squash.

Tomatoes 3 recipes

Tomatoes

Fresh Heirloom Tomato Salsa

Ripe heirloom tomatoes, charred chile, onion, lime and cilantro, chopped by hand and salted to taste. Salsa cruda is the reason to grow more tomatoes than you can eat fresh.

Total 20 min Condiment

Tomatoes

Heirloom Tomato Bruschetta

Grilled country bread rubbed with raw garlic, piled with marinated heirloom tomatoes and torn basil. Four ingredients, and the single best use of a ripe August tomato.

Total 20 min Appetizer

Tomatoes

Skillet-Fried Green Tomatoes

Firm, tart green tomatoes are soaked in seasoned buttermilk, rolled in coarse cornmeal, and seared in cast iron until the crust crackles. A thrifty Southern classic for the end of tomato season.

Total 27 min Side Dish
